# Compatibility

| Android | Status |
|---|---|
| 14 | Developed and tested here. |
| 7.0 to 13, and 15 | Should work. |
| 5.x and 6.x | Not supported: the app doesn't install. |

## Limits
- Picture in picture needs Android 8.0 or later.
- HEVC, 10-bit, HDR and Dolby Vision depend on the phone, not the app.
- Casting finds TVs on the same network only; a VPN that blocks local traffic hides them.
